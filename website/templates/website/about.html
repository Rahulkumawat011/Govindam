Our History

Precision Metal Fabrication Since 2015

Govindam Enterprises was founded with a dedicated focus on manufacturing high-strength steel hardware for overhead power transmission and distribution networks. Our journey began with basic metal press work and has evolved into a state-of-the-art gantry welding and CNC laser facility.

Based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, we serve public sector electricity boards, solar grid developers, and industrial EPC contracting giants, delivering custom components matching precision blueprints.

Our Core Philosophy

Mission

To manufacture reliable, robust, and cost-effective transmission hardware using advanced fabrication technologies, ensuring energy connectivity with safety and speed.

Vision

To become the national standard for steel fabrication quality in the power sector, pioneering zero-defect manufacturing for next-generation green energy grids.
