1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when requesting services or contacting us. This includes your name, mobile number, district, message details, and service choices.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected details to process your utility liaisoning requests, contact you with consultations or updates, and manage the official application submission with electricity departments on your behalf.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell or lease your personal information. Details are only shared with government electricity board departments (such as the State Bijli Board) to process your applications.

4. Security

We employ administrative and technical precautions (including SSL routing and CSRF checks) to protect your contact requests database logs.
